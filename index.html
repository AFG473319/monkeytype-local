about

This is monkeytype local — a self-contained typing test that runs entirely on your machine. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no network request, and no build step. Open index.html and it works, offline, forever.

what it measures

  • wpm — net words per minute: correctly typed characters (plus the spaces after fully correct words) divided by 5, divided by minutes elapsed. A word is only credited when every letter in it is right.
  • raw — every character you pressed, right or wrong, over the same period. The gap between raw and wpm is your error cost.
  • accuracy — correct keystrokes divided by all keystrokes. Fixing a mistake does not undo the wrong press, exactly like the real thing.
  • consistency — 1 minus the coefficient of variation of your per-second raw speed. High consistency means you held a steady pace instead of sprinting and stalling.
  • burst — your fastest one-second window.
  • characters — correct, incorrect, extra (typed past the end of a word) and missed (skipped by pressing space early).

modes

  • time — 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds; the clock decides when you stop.
  • words — 10, 25, 50, 100 or on-and-on; you stop when the last word is finished.
  • quote — short, medium or long passages, punctuation and capitals included.
  • zen — no word list at all. Type whatever you like and press enter when you are done.

keyboard

tabrestart the test, keeping your settings
tab then enterrestart the test (the classic monkeytype gesture)
escopen the command line
ctrl + backspacedelete the whole current word
enternext test, once a result is showing

privacy

Your results, personal bests and settings live in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Clearing site data for this page clears your history, which is the only way to "delete your account".
